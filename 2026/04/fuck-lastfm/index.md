# I'm out from making tags work on Last.fm
I'm finally back after "I don't now how much time", things have been pretty rough lately especially on the "school" side and I know this is a very underwhelming post to do after so long but I really wanted to share my frustrations with Last.fm, the platform I use (or, I guess now used) for scrobbling on my Navidrome server.  
I'm particulary upset with the level of enshittification it reached and how complicated the tags situation on Last.fm really is, this post is purposefully not going to contain any header of subdivision of any type, because my main focus is how complicated is to adjust the tags of every single album for it to work flawlessly on Last.fm.  
There's this first big problem where in Last.fm, unlike alternatives such as ListenBrainz (which I now generally prefer), there's no such thing as "Multiple Artists".
