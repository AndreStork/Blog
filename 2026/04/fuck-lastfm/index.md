# I'm out from making tags work on Last.fm
I'm finally back after "I don't now how much time", things have been pretty rough lately especially on the "school" side and I know this is a very underwhelming post to do after so long but I really wanted to share my frustrations with Last.fm, the platform I use (or, I guess now used) for scrobbling on my Navidrome server.  
I'm particulary upset with the level of **enshittification** it has reached and how complicated the tags situation on Last.fm really is, this post is purposefully not going to contain any header or subdivision of any type, because my main focus today is just how complicated is to adjust the tags of every single album for them to work flawlessly on Last.fm.  
There's this first big problem where in Last.fm, unlike alternatives such as ListenBrainz (which I now generally prefer), there's no such thing as "Multiple Artists": no matter how you put your `ARTIST` tag, Last.fm is always gonna save it as one artist, including the separator.  
This also adds up to the second issue: Last.fm (unlike ListenBrainz which associates scrobbles with a certain MusicBrainz entry) takes the tags as-is which can be an issue if, for example, people and streaming platforms have different ideas on how to handle artist names of, for example different transliterations. In my case in particular where most of my listening interest is on Hardcore and especially the J-Core scene, and as such composed for the most part by Japanese artists, there's for example two ways you can name an artist like "Camellia", which is either in Romaji (Camellia) or, in my case, *hiragana* (かめりあ), Last.fm doesn't have any type of link between these two which can cause stats to fuck up and make unnecessary duplicates.  
Also, third problem, if you ever wanna correct past mistakes caused by wrong tags (which can happen even if your tags were technically correct) you can't fix it in bulk unless you pay up for Last.fm Pro (ewwwwww).  
One example of this huge problem is a conflict between the artist tag of two artists I've encountered: the artist tag of "Ponko2 Girlish" (the song itself, not the whole album) is officialy considered to be "t+pazolite feat. ななひら", meanwhile "Screamin' Showcase" is marked as "t+pazolite & ななひら"; while both of these namings are correct and them being the other way around would technically be wrong, Last.fm would still count them as different and a single artist, which also makes the original "t+pazolite" and "ななひら" not actually raise on scrobble for stats, making them less accurate than they actually are.
